# Branding And Name Usage

The Ultimate OSS Voting Tool (OpaVoting) is released as AGPLv3-or-later software. The license covers the source code and the rights granted by the license.

The project name, logo, visual identity, screenshots, and official branding are separate identity assets. Do not use them to imply official endorsement, affiliation, or a hosted service operated by the original maintainer unless you have explicit written permission from the maintainer.

Forks may accurately describe themselves as forks of this project. Public or paid services based on a fork must use clearly distinct branding and must not present themselves as the official OpaVoting service.

Paid software services, hosted offerings, commercial marketing, or other public commercial use of the official project name, logo, visual identity, screenshots, or official branding requires explicit written permission from the maintainer.
